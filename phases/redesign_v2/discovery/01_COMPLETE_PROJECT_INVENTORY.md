## 1. Executive Summary & Monorepo Topology

This inventory captures **every user-facing screen, route, modal, sheet, dialog, and shared widget** across the entire Orient Workshop monorepo. It details the technical dependencies, state bindings, route contracts, responsive layout profiles, and localization requirements.

The monorepo comprises four production client applications and three shared core packages:
1. **`staff_app`**: Workshop operations hub serving three distinct personas: Service Advisor, Workshop Supervisor, and Workshop Technician.
2. **`owner_app`**: Executive business intelligence, financial oversight, revenue analytics, staff attendance, inventory management, and compliance portal.
3. **`customer_app`**: Consumer vehicle portal covering live service tracking, estimate approvals, 4-step service bookings, roadside assistance, and digital garage management.
4. **`crm_app`**: Customer relationship management, omnichannel conversations, lead kanban/pipeline, sales rep tracking, and marketing integration hub.
5. **`shared_auth`**: Omnichannel authentication module (Password, Email OTP, SMS OTP, Role Redirection, Password Reset).
6. **`shared_core`**: Design system tokens, responsive layout framework, interactive primitives, feedback components, network interceptors, and sync engines.
7. **`shared_models`**: Centralized domain enums (`UserRole`, `JobCardStatus`, etc.).

## 4. Architectural Contracts to Preserve

Throughout the entire Redesign v2 execution, the following engineering foundations **MUST NOT BE BROKEN**:
1. **State Management**: All Riverpod providers (`authNotifierProvider`, `jobCardsProvider`, `bookingWizardProvider`, `crmLeadProvider`, etc.) retain their signatures and state notifier behaviors.
2. **Offline & Persistence**: Hive offline storage boxes (`advisor_intakes`, `customer_bookings`, `offline_checkins`) and sync queue engines must remain 100% operational.
3. **Route Paths & Deep Links**: All canonical route constants (`AppRoutes.*`) and query parameter parsing (`?vehicleId=`, `?estimateId=`, `?tab=`) must be strictly preserved.
4. **Backend Contracts**: All Dio HTTP interceptors, `ApiResponse` envelopes, and DTO schemas in `shared_core/lib/src/network/` and `shared_core/lib/src/models/` remain unmodified.
5. **Localization Infrastructure**: `shared_core` localization delegates, locale providers, and the 100% bilingual requirement (English LTR and Arabic RTL) must be maintained across every single screen.
